🌍 Change the language · Ibdel il-lingwa

→ →

Click Kelma Buddy in the corner of your screen, then press the flag button. Each press changes the language: Malti → English → Italiano. Buddy tells you the new language out loud.

Agħfas fuq il-ħabib Kelma fil-kantuniera tal-iskrin, imbagħad agħfas il-bandiera. Kull darba tinbidel il-lingwa: Malti → English → Italiano.
